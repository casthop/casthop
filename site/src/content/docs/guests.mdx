Guests join from a link. They do not need an account.

## How it works

1. The host opens a studio and copies the guest link.
2. The guest opens the link in a browser and enters a name.
3. The guest waits backstage. They can be seen by the host, but are not in the stream.
4. The host brings them on stage. Up to six people can be on stage at once.

![The guest join screen with a green test picture.](/guest.png)

*The green picture is a fake camera in a test capture, not a real guest.*

## Who can do what

- **Hosting needs an account.** The host signs in with an email magic link, or GitHub if you configured it. The owner of a studio, and any co-host they invite, can control it.
- **Guests cannot impersonate the host.** Identities are assigned by the Worker, and every action is checked on the server.
- **Guests never see stream keys.** The public studio state only includes a masked suffix of each destination URL. See [Destinations](/docs/destinations).

## If a guest cannot connect

TURN for guests behind strict NAT is on the roadmap and not built yet. A guest on a restrictive network may fail to connect for now.
